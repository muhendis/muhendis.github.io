I am an AI engineer working on large language model and machine learning systems at Vodafone, and an AWS Certified Solutions Architect.

My earlier work was in computer vision for digital pathology at Virasoft, where I designed, trained and evaluated deep learning models for object detection and instance segmentation, managed AWS infrastructure for those projects, and helped build an in-house AI data system. That work produced two peer-reviewed papers on breast cancer histopathology.

Alongside engineering I write and teach. I am the author of a Turkish book on end-to-end data science with Python, and my Udemy course on Python for data science has reached more than 20,000 students across 50 countries.
