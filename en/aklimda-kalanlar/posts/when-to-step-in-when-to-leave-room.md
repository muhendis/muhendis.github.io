## Thinking Notes and Starting Questions

This draft is a notebook to explore the nuance of intervention timing in engineering leadership and technical mentorship.

### Core questions I keep asking

- In a moment of crisis or uncertainty, why is my default reflex to step in immediately? Is that reflex serving the team or my own need for control?
- Which mistakes are essential learning opportunities, and which ones genuinely threaten the team or system safety? How do I tell the difference in practice?
- Why does staying quiet while waiting for an engineer to find their own breakthrough often require far more discipline and effort than simply giving the answer?
- Looking back at moments where I intervened too early versus moments where I gave the team real room to lead, what was the defining difference?
